**XXL STRIP** is an emulation of a classic large-format British console channel strip (4000 E / G style): input with drive, high- and low-pass filters, four-band EQ in three models (E Brown, E Black, G), channel compressor, gate / expander, VU meter.

For Akai Force / MPC OS (ARMv7). Unzip and run `install.sh` on the device; `uninstall.sh` removes it. Free.

Tested and running on an Akai Force (L'Cronx, 2026-11-14).
SHA-256 sums: `releases/SHA256SUMS.txt` in the repository.
